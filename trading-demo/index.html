Prediction markets

Trade YES / NO shares on sample events. A share pays $1.00 if its outcome happens and $0 if not, so the price reads as the market-implied probability. Yes/No, multi-outcome and range markets; market and limit orders; prices from an automated market maker (LMSR).

Sample markets Questions and odds are illustrative only, not forecasts, and are not linked to real exchanges or real results. Other traders’ flow is simulated. Use “Resolve (demo)” to settle a market yourself.
